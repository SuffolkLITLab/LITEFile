# State-by-state court help & resources

LITEFile is designed to support electronic court filing across court systems nationwide. When you use LITEFile, the system automatically adapts to your selected state—displaying state-approved court statements, local legal aid contacts, and court help lines in the footer and on your filing status screens.

Find official court help centers, legal aid organizations, and approved form repositories for your state below:

---

## Supported & partner jurisdictions

### 🏛️ Illinois
* **Official court help**: [Illinois Court Help (ilcourthelp.gov)](https://www.ilcourthelp.gov) | Phone: `833-411-1121`
* **Legal aid & guided forms**: [Illinois Legal Aid Online (ILAO)](https://www.illinoislegalaid.org)
* **Approved court forms**: [Illinois Supreme Court Standardized Forms](https://www.illinoiscourts.gov/documents-and-forms/approved-forms)

### 🏛️ Massachusetts
* **Official court help**: [Massachusetts Court Service Centers](https://www.mass.gov/orgs/court-service-centers) | Phone / TTY: `711`
* **Legal aid resources**: [MassLegalHelp](https://www.masslegalhelp.org)
* **Approved court forms**: [Massachusetts Trial Court Forms](https://www.mass.gov/lists/court-forms)

### 🏛️ Vermont
* **Official court help**: [Vermont Judiciary Self-Help Services](https://www.vtcourts.gov/self-help)
* **Legal aid & advocacy**: [Legal Services Vermont (LSV)](https://legalservicesvt.org) | [VTLawHelp](https://vtlawhelp.org)
* **Approved court forms**: [Vermont Court Forms Directory](https://www.vtcourts.gov/court-forms)

## National & multi-state legal tech resources

If your state is not listed above or you are preparing forms across jurisdictions:
* **[LawHelp.org](https://www.lawhelp.org)**: A national directory to find free legal aid programs and legal information in all 50 states.
* **[Legal Services Corporation (LSC)](https://www.lsc.gov/about-lsc/what-legal-aid/get-legal-assistance)**: Find federally funded legal aid offices near you.
* **[Document Assembly Line](https://assemblyline.suffolklitlab.org)**: Guided online interviews and automated form preparation tools developed by the Suffolk University Law School LIT Lab.

---

## How LITEFile connects you to local help

When you file in LITEFile:
1. **Footer court statement & help line**: Every page shows the official court statement and direct telephone assistance for the state you are filing in.
2. **Rejection assistance**: If a court clerk returns a filing, LITEFile displays the exact phone number, email address, and website of that specific court clerk's office so you know whom to contact for clarification.
